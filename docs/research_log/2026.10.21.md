# Research log: week of 2026.10.21

**Status:** result established, including a no-delta control and a run with domain randomization off; both were added on 2026-10-24. Only one training seed per arm so far.

## Question

Does finetuning the m4 base policy against a frozen pelvis-wrench delta improve its Genesis performance? Does that still hold when the delta never saw m4, as long as a gap-model uncertainty gate is applied?

**Short answer:**

- Yes to both questions.
- The delta, not extra PPO iterations, is what transfers the policy to the payload.
- Finetuning with the m1_m2_m3 delta and domain randomization **off** gives the best policy after only **10 iterations**: 100% completion, 26.3 mm local MPJPE and 84.8 mm MPJPE.
- Without the delta, finetuning needs randomization and about 100 iterations to come close, and is still worse.

## 1. Delta finetunes vs base (5 seeds × 100 rollouts)

| Policy (model_30020) | Delta gate | Success | MPJPE (mm) | Local MPJPE (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base policy | – | 72.2% (64–78% across seeds) | 178 | 45.6 |
| Finetuned, m1_m2_m3_m4 delta | none | 99.8% | 123 | 27.5 |
| Finetuned, m1_m2_m3 delta | gap model, λ = 3 | 100% | 121 | 27.3 |

- **Against the base policy:** both finetuned policies beat it (Fisher exact test p ≈ 1e-45 each).
- **Against each other:** they are indistinguishable (p = 1.0).
- **Interpretation:** the delta that never saw m4 works as well as the one that did. So most of the gain comes from correcting the general Isaac–Genesis mismatch, not anything specific to m4.

## 3. Is the payload actually what breaks the base policy?

Yes. With the same protocol at nominal mass, Isaac-to-Genesis transfer is already perfect.

| Policy | Pelvis payload | Completion | MPJPE (mm) | Local MPJPE (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base | none | 100% | 60.9 | 22.4 |
| Base | +10 kg | 73.4% | 174.2 | 46.0 |
| No delta, 100 iterations | none | 100% | 63.7 | 23.5 |
| No delta, 100 iterations | +10 kg | 100% | 111.4 | 28.7 |

Finetuning does not improve tracking at nominal mass; it reduces sensitivity to the payload.

**At +15 kg** (`configs/genesis/pelvis_extra15kg.yaml`, 2 seeds × 100 rollouts), the no-delta control breaks down:

| Policy | Completion | Clip survival | Median time to failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| No delta, 100 iterations | 13.5% | ~58% | ~11 s |
| Base | 0% | ~22% | ~4 s |

The delta-finetuned policies have not been tested at +15 kg yet.

## Conclusions

1. **The delta is what transfers the policy to the payload.** With the delta and no randomization, 10 iterations give

   100% completion and 26.3 mm local MPJPE. Without the delta, 100 randomized iterations reach only 28.7 mm. That is
     about 10× fewer finetuning iterations, and a better end result.
2. **Randomization is not needed with the delta, and slightly hurts.** Turning it off cuts global MPJPE by 30%

   (120.5 → 84.8 mm) and improves every other metric slightly. A likely reason is that randomization adds perturbations
     unrelated to the payload, which make tracking more conservative.
3. **Why plain finetuning helped at all:** randomization, through pushes and torso centre-of-mass shifts, happens to cover

   part of a payload's effect, but only up to about +10 kg. At +15 kg the no-delta control fails.
4. **Data-scale study:**
   - Use the configuration below as the reference.
   - Measure local and global MPJPE.
   - Completion can't be used, because it is saturated at +10 kg for every finetuned arm.

## Caveats

- **One training seed per arm.** The tight rollout-level intervals don't capture variation between training runs.
- **Delta finetunes tested only at the +10 kg payload they were trained on.**
- **Later checkpoints may not hold up.** The torso-payload finetune ([2026.10.28](2026.10.28.md)) was excellent at 20
iterations and collapsed by 40. Here the no-randomization run held at 10–30 iterations over 100 rollouts.
- **A corrupted results file.** `m1234_delta_model_30020/metrics.json` contains a one-token write glitch
(`"step""step"25`). Readers repair it in memory; completion and tracking values are unaffected.
