# Research log: week of 2026.11.18

**Status:** in progress (started 2026-11-12, last updated 2026-11-16).

**Goal:** iterative, failure-free sim-to-real through a motion curriculum. Collect safe data on m_k, train a delta,
finetune for m_{k+1}, deploy without falls ([`project_understanding.md`](../project_understanding.md)). Genesis stands in
for the real robot.

**Setup:** pelvis +12.5 kg ([`pelvis_extra12.5kg.yaml`](../../configs/genesis/pelvis_extra12.5kg.yaml)). Chain
([`CHAIN.md`](../../artifacts/genesis/2026.11.18/CHAIN.md)): m0 = walk1_subject2 140–160 s, m1 = walk1_subject1 20–40 s,
m2 = walk1_subject1 0–20 s.

## Key result

**On m1 and m2, finetuning the base with a gap-gated delta (λ = 10) tracks better than plain finetuning.** The delta comes
from the previous steps of the chain: m0 + m1 safe for m1, and m0 + m1 (rollouts of delta finetune 30016) for m2. Local
MPJPE, velocity error and acceleration error are lower at almost every checkpoint, and the gap grows with iterations.

Protocol for both motions:
- runs start from the base policy, Isaac DR off, injection 0.5, 20 checkpoints (30000–30019);
- the control is the same finetune without the delta;
- each checkpoint is evaluated on the full clip in Genesis at +12.5 kg, with 100 rollouts at seed 0, paired by start state;
- the confidence intervals come from a two-level bootstrap (checkpoints, then rollouts).

Delta − control is shown with its 95% confidence interval; "wins" counts the checkpoints that favour the delta.

| Metric | m1, all 20 checkpoints | m1, 30010–30019 | m2, all 20 checkpoints | m2, 30010–30019 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MPJPE local (mm) | **−2.4** [−3.6, −1.3], 16/20 wins | **−4.5** [−5.5, −3.4], 10/10 | **−3.4** [−4.1, −2.7], 19/20 | **−4.1** [−4.8, −3.3], 10/10 |
| Velocity error | **−0.42** [−0.61, −0.22] | **−0.73** | **−0.51** [−0.61, −0.40] | **−0.64** |
| MPJPE global (mm) | −3.3 [−10.3, +3.4] | **−14.2** [−23.5, −4.9] | +2.8 [−1.2, +7.1] | +4.3 [−1.6, +11.1] |
| Falls (of 2000 / 1000) | 9 vs 13 | **0 vs 10** | 34 vs 41 | 0 vs 1 |

Base completion is 90% on m1 and 79% on m2. Both finetunes complete close to 100% from about 30005.

![m2 delta vs control](../../logs/delta_finetune/2026.11.18/m2_compare_delta_vs_control_it20_20261116/per_checkpoint.png)

- **Local tracking and smoothness:** the delta's gain is consistent on both motions.
  - The best local MPJPE is 24.7 mm on m2 (control 30.6) and 26.3 mm on m1 (control 32.5).
  - The delta run is no better at the first checkpoints: on m1 it is slightly worse until about 30004, and m2 30000 completes
    only 71%.
- **Global MPJPE:** the delta helps only on m1's second half, where the control drifts to 140–160 mm. On m2 the effect is
  inconclusive: both runs swing by ±20 mm between checkpoints.
- **Falls:** they don't separate the two runs. Plain finetuning alone removes the payload falls on both motions (m1 from
  30001; m2 from 30005).
- **Plots:** m1 is in [`per_checkpoint.png`](../../logs/delta_finetune/2026.11.18/m1_compare_delta_vs_control_it20_20261116/per_checkpoint.png),
  and the effect plots for both motions are in `aggregate.png` in the same folders.

**Caveats:**
- One finetune seed per run and one Genesis seed. The bootstrap covers variation across checkpoints and rollouts, not across
  finetune seeds.
- For m1, the delta's 30000–30008 come from the 10-iteration run. Its weights are near-identical to the 20-iteration run's
  (max |Δw| 0.006) but not the same. The no-delta runs reproduce exactly.

## How we got here

1. **Payload and chain.** Base completion of m0 / m1 / m2 (100 rollouts):

   | Payload | m0 | m1 | m2 |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | +12 kg | 95% | 100% | 98% |
   | +12.5 kg | 96% | 91% | 79% |
   | +13 kg | 96% | 89% | 69% |
   | +14 kg | 83% | 30% | – |

   I picked +12.5 kg for a success gap that rises along the chain. Folders are in
   [`logs/sim2sim_eval/2026.11.18/`](../../logs/sim2sim_eval/2026.11.18/).
2. **Data (seed 0).** m0: 20 × 20 s, used as safe data despite the 96% rate. m1: 15 × 20 s (oracle only). m2: 10 × 20 s, one
   fall at 7.2 s. **m1 safe prefix:** 5 × 5 s, 0 falls. m1 falls cluster at about 8 s.
3. **MuJoCo as a payload-agnostic screen.** The evaluator is now batched with DR: friction, mass, kp/kd, torso CoM and pushes
   ([`scripts/sim2sim_mujoco/`](../../scripts/sim2sim_mujoco/)). m1 completion is 99% with ±0.5 m/s pushes and 92% with
   ±1.0 m/s. The falls are push-driven and don't flag Genesis's 8 s failure. It isn't useful for picking a safe horizon yet.
   Folders: [`m1_mujoco_dr_*`](../../logs/sim2sim_eval/2026.11.18/).
4. **m0 delta (5k it): good on m0, harmful on m1.** Open loop at 1 s, body position error:
   - held-out m0: 6.8 cm with the delta, 44.4 cm with zero delta;
   - m1 prefix: 57 cm with the delta, 31 cm with zero delta.

   The m0 gap ensemble finds m1 fully out of distribution (AUROC 1.0, R² < 0). Gating only neutralizes the delta: λ = 10
   gives roughly zero-delta position error and a small orientation gain.
   Folders: [`m0_val_m0delta_openloop_1s_20261113`](../../logs/delta_eval/2026.11.18/m0_val_m0delta_openloop_1s_20261113/),
   [`m1_safe5s_m0delta_openloop_1s_20261113`](../../logs/delta_eval/2026.11.18/m1_safe5s_m0delta_openloop_1s_20261113/).
5. **m0 + m1-prefix delta (20 m0 + 5 m1 × 5 s, 5k it) transfers.** Open loop, body position error at 1 s:
   - fresh m1, first 5 s: 4.0 cm with the delta, 31.3 cm with zero delta (wins 100% of windows);
   - unseen 5–8 s: 11.5 cm with the delta, 27.6 cm with zero delta.

   Gated λ = 10 is best at short horizons on the unseen segment. The merged gap ensemble covers m1 (u 0.108, R² 0.31–0.44).

   ![open loop fresh m1](../../logs/delta_eval/2026.11.18/m1_fresh5s_seed1_openloop_1s_20261114/error_vs_horizon.png)

   On the 5–8 s segment: [plot](../../logs/delta_eval/2026.11.18/m1_5to8s_seed2_openloop_1s_20261114/error_vs_horizon.png).
6. **Finetunes with the m0-only delta add nothing.** Gated λ = 10, from the base or from control 30003:
   - on the 5 s prefix, all checkpoints complete 100% and the delta's gain over the control is at most marginal;
   - on the 5–8 s segment, they fall at about 6 s (up to 5 / 100), while the control has none.

   Folders: [`m1_m0delta_gap10_noDR_save1_20261113`](../../logs/delta_finetune/2026.11.18/m1_m0delta_gap10_noDR_save1_20261113/),
   [`m1_ctrl30003_m0delta_gap10_noDR_save1_20261113`](../../logs/delta_finetune/2026.11.18/m1_ctrl30003_m0delta_gap10_noDR_save1_20261113/),

## Code changes

- **MuJoCo evaluator is batched and has DR.** The evaluator is
  [`eval_sim2sim_mujoco.py`](../../scripts/eval_sim2sim_mujoco.py), and the DR ranges are in
  [`domain_randomization.py`](../../scripts/sim2sim_mujoco/domain_randomization.py).
  - Batching: per-env models stepped in threads, with affine PD actuators.
  - DR: friction, mass, kp/kd, torso CoM and interval pushes.
  - Termination and metrics match Genesis.
  - The camera matches Genesis.
