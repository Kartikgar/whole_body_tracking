## Key result

**A pelvis-wrench delta, trained on the policy's own Genesis data, improves a policy beyond what DR finetuning reaches on an
asymmetric arm payload.** m4 (Fast Walking), Genesis, +10 kg on the left upper arm:

| Policy                                                                                     | Rollouts | Completion            | Global MPJPE (mm) | Local MPJPE (mm) | Vel / accel dist |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------- | --------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- |
| Base (`lafan_walk1_subject1/model_29999`)                                                  | 100      | 14%                   | 422               | 63.1             | —                |
| `model_30050`: DR-only finetune of base, no payload knowledge                              | 500      | 72.6% (68.5–76.5)     | 317               | 52.5             | 7.71 / 2.23      |
| `model_30056`**:** `model_30050` **+ 6 iterations, delta (seed 2) gap-gated λ = 4, DR on** | 500      | **91.0% (88.1–93.4)** | 332               | **48.7**         | **7.34 / 2.17**  |
| Control: `model_30050` + DR-only, iterations 1–10 (mean of 10 ckpts)                       | 10 × 100 | 63.3% (60.2–66.3)     | 331               | 53.9             | —                |
| Delta run, iterations 1–10 (mean of 10 ckpts)                                              | 10 × 100 | **83.6% (81.2–85.8)** | 321               | **49.5**         | —                |

- 30050 → 30056, identical 500 start states: McNemar p = 2 × 10⁻¹⁴ (122 rollouts rescued, 30 lost). Local MPJPE −3.6 mm (median paired), vel/accel also better (all p < 10⁻¹⁸). Global MPJPE +9 mm overall, but equal on the 333 rollouts both complete (326.5 vs 328.1, p = 0.73). The delta removes 30050's main failure at 8–11 s (120 → 19 falls).
- The DR-only control from the same start does not improve (it swings 50–84%); the delta run beats it at 9/10 checkpoints (completion, Wilcoxon p = 0.010) and 10/10 on local MPJPE (p = 0.002).
- Success vs finetuning iteration, DR + delta vs DR-only control (**single seed only**: finetune seed 0, delta seed 2;
100 rollouts per point): the delta run stays at 84–93% from iteration 4, the control swings 50–84% and ends at 57%.

## How we got here

1. **New gap scenarios** (Genesis configs in `configs/genesis/`): left upper arm and left thigh payloads on the roll links,

   low friction, pelvis payloads. Base policy on m4: arm 7/8/9/10 kg → 100/97/73/14% complete; thigh 9 kg → 14%;
    friction 0.2/0.3/0.4 → 0/0/100%. On m6 (Running) arm 9 kg, thigh 9 kg and friction 0.3 all give 0–2%.
    Datasets (100 rollouts each): [`m4_scenarios/`](../../artifacts/genesis/2026.11.11/m4_scenarios/),
    [`m6_scenarios/`](../../artifacts/genesis/2026.11.11/m6_scenarios/); deltas for all 6 (5k it, seed 0) in
    `logs/rsl_rl/2026.11.11/g1_delta_wrench_policies/`.
2. **9 kg arm with the base policy: delta finetuning failed.** Open loop the delta beats zero delta at every horizon

   ([`m4_arm9kg_openloop_gap1_20261106`](../../logs/delta_eval/2026.11.11/m4_arm9kg_openloop_gap1_20261106/)), but every
    delta-gated finetune (λ = 1/3/4, DR on/off) collapsed below the base (0–9% by iteration 20–40), while the DR-only finetune
    of the base reached 100% (this became `model_30050`). Closed loop, Isaac+delta was harsher than Genesis (falls at ~14.5 s;
    [videos](../../logs/delta_eval/2026.11.11/m4_arm9kg_gap3_closedloop_videos_20261106/),
    [λ sweep](../../logs/delta_eval/2026.11.11/m4_arm9kg_gap_lambda_sweep_100env_20261106/)). Saving every iteration showed
    the gain is real but brief: DR off peaks at 88% (it. 5) then collapses at it. 8; DR on holds 91–95% through it. 8.
3. **10 kg arm from** `model_30050`**: fidelity check.** DR-only 30050 generalizes to 10 kg (62.8% pooled over 4 seeds vs 14%

   base). Isaac+delta vs Genesis from matched starts, λ = 2–5.5
    ([`m4_ft30050_arm10kg_d2_gap4_100env_20261107`](../../logs/delta_eval/2026.11.11/m4_ft30050_arm10kg_d2_gap4_100env_20261107/)):
    local MPJPE matches Genesis only at λ ≈ 2.5, where global drift is 2× Genesis; drift matches only at λ ≥ 5.5, where local
    error is 30% low. No λ reproduces Genesis's falls at 8.5 s; per-rollout correlation ≈ 0. A root wrench produces too much
    drift per unit of pose error for a limb payload.
4. **10 kg arm delta finetune vs DR-only control:** the key result above.
5. **Pelvis control (in progress).** `model_30050` at +10/11/12 kg pelvis completes 100/94/44%; 12 kg chosen to roughly

   match the arm's difficulty (it is harder: 44% vs 72%). Delta (seed 0, 5k it):
    [`*_m4_ft30050_pelvis12kg_100_rsf_w-1.0_hzn10.0_it5k_sd0`](../../logs/rsl_rl/2026.11.11/g1_delta_wrench_policies/).
6. **Open loop, a root delta matches a pelvis payload better than an arm payload.** 5k-it deltas (arm seed 2, pelvis
   seed 0), 100 held-out rollouts of 30050 per payload (seed 1), 2 s windows. Residual error with the delta, 0.25 → 2 s:
   body position 7.3 → 11.2 cm (arm) vs 2.3 → 6.1 cm (pelvis); body orientation ≈ 0.30 vs ≈ 0.10 rad at every horizon.
   Both beat zero delta in > 90% of windows. One delta seed per payload; payload difficulty not matched.

## Fauna Sprout: Phase 1 (loading, standing, PD)

- Sprout spec, Isaac config and derived stand pose:
[`sprout_spec.py`](../../source/whole_body_tracking/whole_body_tracking/robots/sprout_spec.py),
[`sprout.py`](../../source/whole_body_tracking/whole_body_tracking/robots/sprout.py),
[`sprout_stand_pose.json`](../../source/whole_body_tracking/whole_body_tracking/robots/sprout_stand_pose.json).
- With the hardware gains (kp 32.5 / 10, kd 1) Sprout cannot stand on PD alone: it falls backwards at about 1.3 s in
MuJoCo (1.32 s), Genesis (1.33 s) and Isaac (1.27 s). Videos and logs:
[`stand_nominal/`](../../logs/sprout_phase1/stand_nominal/).
- **Open issue, velocity limit (Isaac only).** `SPROUT_CFG` sets `velocity_limit_sim` to the URDF placeholder 5 rad/s on
every joint. Isaac enforces it; MuJoCo and Genesis enforce no joint velocity limit. The slow stand fall likely stayed below
it, but tracking motions will exceed 5 rad/s, so Isaac would clamp joints that Genesis does not, which would appear as a
false sim-to-sim dynamics gap in delta data. Before recording Sprout delta data, either drop the limit in Isaac or apply
the same (datasheet) limit in all simulators. Torque limits (80 Nm placeholder) need the same check.
Deferred to policy training/evaluation (decision 2026-11-09). When changing them:
  - Omitting `effort_limit_sim` / `velocity_limit_sim` does **not** remove the limits: implicit actuators fall back to
  the asset values, which the URDF converter copies from the URDF (80 Nm, 5 rad/s). Set large values explicitly
  (e.g. `1e9` / `1e3`). Removing them does not destabilize the implicit PD (max drive torque ≈ kp × range ≈ 150 Nm).
  - `SPROUT_ACTION_SCALE = 0.25 × effort_limit / kp` (G1 formula) depends on the effort limit; fix it to the current
  values (0.62 rad lower body, 2.0 rad upper body) before raising the limit.
  - MuJoCo (Fauna MJCF) has no force or velocity limit; `scripts/sprout/stand_nominal_genesis.py` sets a ±80 Nm force
  range. Keep the three simulators consistent.
  - Retargeting does not use the URDF velocity limit: frax enforced it and saturated 19% of LAFAN1 frames at 5 rad/s,
  so the Sprout retargeter uses a 30 rad/s cap (`RETARGET_V_MAX` in `scripts/sprout/retarget/sprout_frax.py`).
- Also open: waist kp is 32.5 in the MJCF but 10 in the SDK example walk policy; the sims use 32.5.

- The evaluation probe (`--eprobe_share`; probe worlds share the training scene, training worlds snapshotted /
restored exactly around each sweep) gives no measurable gain on Isaac (±0.012 per motion type, single seed) at 2.3×

- Isaac ≥ mjlab on every motion type except walking (−0.003); largest gain on fall-and-get-up (+0.045 at 3.5k).
Isaac per-iteration learning curves track mjlab closely (it 300: ep_len 332 vs 319; it 1000: return 0.681 vs 0.648).
- Isaac 3.8 it/s vs mjlab 8 it/s (no CUDA-graph simulator step on PhysX). Isaac evaluation is deterministic.

How we got here:

1. **Retargeting (frax).** PyRoki was ~1.3 frames/s on CPU (and needs `jaxls@5f766d3`); frax differential IK does all

   of LAFAN1 (497k frames) in ~6 s. Sprout config ported from frax's G1 one (GMR weights; rotation offsets via a
    shared arms-down reference pose; scales from Sprout kinematics; auto-spherized collision model). The URDF's 5 rad/s
    velocity limit throttled 19% of frames, so retargeting uses a 30 rad/s cap. Floor penetration p95 = 0.0 mm (one
    74 mm hip-yaw-flip frame in fightAndSports1_subject1). Output: `data/lafan1_frax/sprout/`; the 40 G1-benchmark clips
    are linked in `data/lafan1_frax/sprout_bm40/`. Scripts: `scripts/sprout/retarget/`.
2. **BlinkMimic on G1 reproduced** (seed 0, 12.4 min incl. a one-time 3.5 min kernel compile).
3. **Sprout in BlinkMimic (mjlab).** New `sprout/` robot package; generalized G1-only code: per-robot feet and contact

   height (0.103 m), and **mirror signs from joint axes** (the roll/yaw name rule mirrors 10 Sprout arm joints wrongly;
    the axis rule reproduces G1's exactly).
4. **Isaac Lab port** (`source/.../blinkmimic/`, `scripts/blinkmimic/`): learner unchanged; Isaac `TrackingSim`; local

   Muon (torch 2.5; matches torch 2.9 to 2e-7). `SPROUT_CFG`: no velocity limit (1000 rad/s explicit), 80 Nm, no
    self-collision, action scale fixed to 80 Nm. Bugs fixed on the way:
   - Isaac Lab 2.1 `root_link_vel_w` is stale right after `write_root_link_velocity_to_sim` (0.2–0.6 error; Sprout's
   torso CoM is 0.18 m off the link origin): root link velocity is computed from the CoM velocity.
   - CUDA-graph capture of the policy update fails intermittently (PhysX launches on the legacy stream from native
   threads): `--no_policy_graph` (eager policy update, no measurable slowdown on Isaac).
   - Isaac Kit's `app.close()` hangs after standalone runs: scripts exit with `os._exit(0)`.
